Responsive behavior

One system, two rhythms

Do not simply shrink a desktop page—recompose its hierarchy.

Web / 12-column discipline

Preserve generous outer margins and group related content in architectural panels. Long-form data can occupy two-thirds; actions stay anchored at the edge of a cluster.

Mobile / decisive stacking

Reduce navigation to the essential, stack panels in task order, and keep actions within thumb reach. Never reduce text below 14px for reading or targets below 44px.
